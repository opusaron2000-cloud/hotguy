Are you in immediate danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger,
seek help from the nearest hospital or emergency
service immediately.

Do not wait for an appointment through this website
when urgent assisstance is needed.

When to Seek Urgent Help

  • When someone might hurt themselves or another person
  • When someone is experiencing a serious mental health crisis.
  • When someone cant keep themselves safe.
  • When someone needs immediate medical attention.

Where to Get Help

Nearest Hospital

Visit the nearest hospital or emergency department
if immediate medical attention is required.

University Counselling Office

Contact the University's approved counselling office
for available mental health support and referral guidance.
