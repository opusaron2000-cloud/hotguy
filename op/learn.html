Mental Health Education

Mental healthis important for every student.
Understanding mental health can help you recognize signs of stress, anxiety, and depression, and seek appropriate support when needed.

1. Stress

Stress is the feeling of pressure or tension when some one faces difficult situations.
It can be caused by academic workload, social pressures, or personal challenges.

Ways to manage stress:

  • Prepare a proper study timetable.
  • Take regular breaks during study sessions.
  • Get enough sleep
  • Talk to someone you trust.

2. Anxiety

Anxiety involves feeling worry, fear or nervousness.
Students may experience anxiety because of examinations,
presentations or uncertainty about the future.

Helpful practices include:

  • Practicing relaxation techniques.
  • Preparing early for examinations.
  • Talking to a trusted person.
  • Seeking professional support when needed.

3.Depression

Depression is a mental health condition that can affect
a persons feelings, thoughts and daily activities.
Anyone experiencing persistent difficulties
should consider seeking proffessional help.

4. Loneliness and Home sickness

Some students may feel lonely when they move
away from home or find it difficult to make
new groups.

  • Join student activities.
  • Keep in touch with family and friends.
  • Participants in support groups.

5. Academic Pressure

Academic pressure can lead to stress and anxiety.
Students may feel overwhelmed by expectations
from themselves, family or peers.

6. Self- Care and Coping tips

  • Eat a balanced diet.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Practice mindfulness or meditation.
  • Set realistic goals and prioritize tasks.

Educational information provided on this page is for general awareness and does not replace professional medical advice.
